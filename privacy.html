Privacy notice.

Last updated 4 November 2026

younitedsnacks.com is the website of YOUNITED Gourmet Snacking. This notice explains what happens to the details you share here. It is short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

YOUNITED Gourmet Snacking is responsible for the personal data processed through this website. You can reach us at kadir@biteandmore.com.

What we collect and why

  • Your enquiry. When you send an enquiry, we receive your name and email address, the role you choose (for example distributor or retailer), the product families you tick, your message and, if you add them, your company and your country or market. We use these only to reply to you and, if we do business together, to manage that relationship. We rely on your request and on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.
  • Your visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or social media pixels.

Who receives it

Your enquiry reaches our team by email, either through your own email app or through Web3Forms, a form delivery service that forwards it to our inbox. We do not sell your details or share them for marketing. Some of these service providers process data outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply and to handle any business that follows. Enquiries that do not lead to business are deleted within 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing kadir@biteandmore.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
